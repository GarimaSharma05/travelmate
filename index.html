About TravelMate

Made with love, pastel stickers, and warm cups of tea.

Travel happy! ✨

Hello, fellow wanderer! 🎒🌸

TravelMate was created to bring back the pure charm of physical scrapbooks, cute journals, and pastel sticky notes into the digital era.

Instead of cold spreadsheets and endless stressful booking tabs, we believe travel planning should feel warm, joyful, and creative — like decorating a personalized travel diary before embarking on your journey.

🎀 Pastel & Playful: Aesthetic cards and hand-drawn doodles.
🧘‍♀️ Stress-Free: Simplified daily plans, packing lists, and coin trackers.
💾 No Login Required: Pure client-side instant functionality.
